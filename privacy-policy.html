Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Waypoint ("we", "us", "our") is a personal travel content project published at hellowaypoint.com, run by José Maria Garcia as part of OpenForYou.org. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the site, why we collect it, who else is involved, and what choices you have.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address (and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares with our sign-in service)When you create an account or sign inTo let you sign in and keep your data safe

You don't need an account to browse Waypoint. You only need one to save your map and trips. We never ask for more personal information than the above.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for the newsletter and analytics cookies, and on providing the service you asked for (your account and saved trips).

Accounts and saved trips

Sign-in and storage are provided by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Your saved data is protected by security rules so that only the account that owns it can read or change it. You can edit or delete any trip at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything saved in it, write to us at the address below.

The Documents section is only meant for short reminders, such as "Passport valid until 2029". Please don't enter passport, ID or card numbers there.

Shared trips

If you switch on "Share trip", we publish a read-only copy of that trip at a link with a random ID. Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with people you trust. The copy includes the trip name, country, dates, stops, places, stays, meals, activities, transport details (never booking references) and your day notes, plus an internal account ID that is not your email. It does not include your email address or your Documents reminders. The copy is updated when you press Save, and it is deleted when you switch sharing off.

Newsletter (Beehiiv)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored in our database (Google Cloud Firestore) and is shared with our newsletter provider, Beehiiv, which sends the emails on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter email, or by writing to us at the address below. We do not sell or share your email address with anyone else.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic to the site: which countries are most viewed, roughly where visitors are browsing from, and how people move through the site. This is done using cookies and similar technologies. The data is aggregated and is not used by us to identify you personally. Google's own privacy practices for this data are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics only load if you accept cookies in the banner shown on your first visit.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using your browser's private/incognito mode or an ad-blocking extension.

Partner links

Some links on Waypoint may be affiliate links, for example to Booking.com, Skyscanner or GetYourGuide. If you use one and book something, the partner may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Partner links are always labelled, and we explain how this works on our How we earn page.

  • A partner link opens the partner's own site in a new tab. From that moment the partner is responsible for your data, and its privacy policy applies.
  • The partner, or the affiliate network it uses, may set cookies or use similar technologies on its own sites to know a booking came from us. That happens on their sites, not ours.
  • When you use a partner search button inside your trip planner, the link may include the city and dates of that stop so the search is pre-filled. We don't send your name, your email or the rest of your trip.
  • We don't ask partners for personal details about you. We only see that a booking was made through our link and the commission it earned.

Cookies and similar technologies

Waypoint uses cookies set by Google Analytics, only if you accept them, to distinguish returning visitors from new ones and to measure site usage. Your browser's storage is also used for things the site needs to work: keeping you signed in, and remembering your cookie choice. We do not use cookies for advertising or to build a profile of you for marketing purposes. If you click a partner link, the partner's site may set its own cookies (see Partner links). You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from browsing the site, though you may need to sign in again and some analytics features may not work as intended.

Your rights

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask for a copy
  • Ask us to correct or delete that data
  • Ask us to restrict or object to how we use it
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example, by unsubscribing from the newsletter)
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at the email address below and we'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children's privacy

Waypoint is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves — for example, if we add new features that collect data differently. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to jose@openforyou.org.

This policy is written in plain language to be genuinely useful, not just legally defensive. If anything here is unclear, feel free to write in and ask.
